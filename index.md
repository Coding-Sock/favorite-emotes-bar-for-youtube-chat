# Favorite Emotes Bar for YouTube Chat Add-on

# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17th, 2026

Favorite Emotes Bar for YouTube Chat (“the extension”) helps you save and use
favorite emotes in YouTube live chat.

## Information stored by the extension

The extension stores favorites and preferences in your browser’s extension
storage. This can include Unicode emoji, custom emote shortcuts and image URLs,
the custom-emote allowlist, and display preferences.

Global favorites, the allowlist, and display preferences may use your browser’s
sync storage when available. If browser sync is enabled, your browser provider
may sync this information across your devices according to its own settings and
privacy policy. The developer does not operate or receive data from that sync
service.

Channel-specific favorites are stored locally in your browser and are not
automatically synced.

## Information the developer receives

The extension does not send analytics, chat messages, browsing history, or
stored favorites to developer-operated servers. It reads the relevant YouTube
live-chat interface to find and insert emotes you choose; this processing
happens in your browser.

When you export a backup, the extension creates a JSON file at your request.
You choose where to save or transfer that file. The extension does not upload it
to the developer.

## Control and deletion

You can edit or remove favorites, delete a channel-specific list, or use the
reset option to clear the extension’s saved settings. Exported backup files are
separate copies and must be deleted by you.
